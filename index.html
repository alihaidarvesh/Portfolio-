Cultural Institution 2024

Al-Noor Cultural Center

Creative Direction, Concept Development, Visual System Design

Overview

A comprehensive brand identity for a contemporary Islamic cultural center aiming to bridge traditional values with modern educational approaches. The institution required a visual language that honors heritage while speaking to younger generations.

The Challenge

Traditional Islamic institutions often face an identity crisis—appearing either too conservative to engage youth or too modern to maintain authenticity. The center needed an identity that could exist confidently in both worlds.

The Concept

The identity is built around the concept of "continuous light"—نور مستمر—representing knowledge as an eternal, flowing presence. The mark utilizes geometric principles from Islamic architecture, specifically the octagonal star pattern, simplified into a contemporary symbol that functions as both a standalone mark and a modular system.

Visual System

Typography pairs contemporary Arabic type with clean Latin equivalents. The color system references traditional Islamic color theory—deep indigo representing night sky and contemplation, gold representing knowledge and enlightenment, balanced with generous white space to create a modern, breathing environment.

EdTech Platform 2024

Ilm Learning Platform

Creative Direction, Brand Strategy, Art Direction

Overview

Rebranding an established Arabic language learning platform to expand into wider Middle Eastern markets. The platform needed to shed its dated aesthetic while maintaining trust with existing users.

The Challenge

The existing brand felt corporate and disconnected from the joy of learning. User research revealed that learners associated the brand with obligation rather than discovery. We needed to inject humanity and curiosity into every touchpoint.

The Concept

Rather than depicting learning as a linear path, we embraced the Arabic word "Ilm" (knowledge) as an expanding universe. The visual system uses organic, expanding forms that suggest growth and discovery—knowledge as something alive and evolving rather than fixed and academic.

Financial Services 2023

Amana Financial Group

Creative Direction, Visual Identity Design

Overview

Creating a brand identity for a new Islamic finance institution that challenges the conservative aesthetic typical of the sector. The client sought to attract a younger, digitally-native audience while maintaining credibility.

The Concept

The concept revolves around "structural trust"—combining the solidity and geometric precision of traditional Islamic patterns with contemporary minimalism. The mark is constructed from a single continuous line, representing integrity, transparency, and the interconnected nature of ethical finance.

Cultural Campaign 2024

Heritage Month Campaign

Art Direction, Campaign Concept

Overview

A government-sponsored campaign celebrating UAE Heritage Month, designed to engage young Emiratis in understanding their cultural roots through contemporary visual storytelling.

The Concept

Instead of nostalgic imagery, we created a visual language that positions heritage as "foundation, not museum." The campaign uses bold typography overlaying archival imagery, with contemporary Arabic calligraphy that bridges generations. Each piece asks: "What did they build? What will you build?"

Publishing 2023

Qalam Publishing House

Brand Identity, Art Direction

Overview

Identity for an independent Arabic publishing house specializing in contemporary literature and translated works. The publisher needed to distinguish itself in a market dominated by traditional academic presses.

The Concept

"Qalam" (قلم) means pen—the identity celebrates the tool itself as both ancient and contemporary. The mark is built from the negative space of a calligraphic stroke, creating a modern symbol from classical gesture. The visual system allows each book to have its own voice while maintaining brand coherence.

Technology 2024

Majlis Connect

Creative Direction, Brand Development

Overview

Brand identity for a community-building platform designed to digitize the traditional Majlis (gathering) concept for remote and hybrid Emirati communities globally.

The Concept

The identity translates the circular, inclusive nature of physical Majlis gatherings into a digital-native visual language. The symbol represents multiple perspectives converging—a modern interpretation of collective wisdom. The color palette references traditional textiles while feeling contemporary and tech-forward.

Background & Artistic Foundation

My approach to brand direction and visual identity is informed by a deep foundation in fine arts and visual storytelling. As a National Award-winning cartoonist, I developed an understanding of how imagery communicates complex narratives with clarity and emotional resonance.

This background in narrative art, combined with formal study in Arabic calligraphy and Islamic visual traditions, allows me to bring conceptual depth and cultural intelligence to contemporary brand challenges. I see design not as decoration, but as a structured visual language—one that can carry meaning, build equity, and create lasting cultural value.

My work synthesizes traditional visual principles with modern communication needs, resulting in identities that feel both timeless and contemporary, rooted yet forward-thinking.
